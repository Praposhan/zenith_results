Our method

Measure first. Then change one thing at a time.

Most sleep advice starts with tips. Ours starts with your data, finds the cause, and only then changes what you do.

The four levers

Where sleep problems come from.

Every pattern we see sits under one of four levers. We name one primary driver, at most two, so you get a plan rather than a list.

Timing

Body clock vs calendar. Signals: a big weekday-to-weekend gap, sleeping well only when your schedule is free.

Drive

Sleep pressure. Signals: long time in bed, low sleep efficiency, naps and "catching up".

Calm

A system that won't switch off. Signals: a long time to fall asleep, a racing mind, a raised overnight heart rate.

Inputs

Daily choices. Signals: late caffeine, evening alcohol, screens in bed, late heavy meals.

Two reference points

Healthy, and improving.

"Normal for you" isn't always healthy. So every measure is read against the right reference.

CLINICAL NORMS

Is it healthy?

Sleep duration, sleep efficiency, time to fall asleep, time awake in the night and regularity are compared with published adult norms, such as at least 7 hours and 85% efficiency. If your usual pattern sits outside them, that is the finding.

YOUR OWN BASELINE

Is it changing?

Heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and temperature vary too much between people to compare. We compare them only with your own 14-night baseline, and track the direction over time.

How we coach

Methods with strong evidence and few side effects.

  • CBT-I-informed coaching: finding your right sleep window and rebuilding the link between bed and sleep.
  • ACT-informed coaching: taking the effort and struggle out of sleep.
  • Body-clock coaching: light, wake time and activity timing.
  • Breathwork, yoga and mindfulness: helping a busy system switch off.
  • Weekly data reviews: progress measured, not guessed.

Honest about limits

We coach. We don't diagnose.

PRAPOSHAN doesn't diagnose conditions, prescribe medication or advise on supplement doses. When something needs a doctor, we say so and point you to the right one.

About your wearable

A sleep tracker works like a step counter for sleep: excellent at trends and patterns over time. Because it reads movement and pulse rather than brain waves, it guides habits; it doesn't diagnose. That's why we read it alongside how you feel, and why a wearable is optional.
