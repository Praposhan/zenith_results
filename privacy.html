Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

PRAPOSHAN ("we", "us") provides sleep and performance coaching. PRAPOSHAN is run by Geethika Babu in Chennai, India. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: praposhanppc@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email, WhatsApp number, city and time zone.
  • Assessment and coaching information: your ZENITH Quiz answers (including questions about sleep, mood and breathing), intake form answers, morning check-ins, and notes from your sessions. Some of this is health information, and we treat it with extra care.
  • Wearable data, only if you choose to share it, such as sleep and heart-rate data from Oura, Apple Watch, Whoop or Garmin.
  • Payment records: payments are processed by Razorpay or PayPal. We receive confirmation of payment; we never see or store your card details.
  • Website usage: basic visit statistics through Google Analytics, with IP addresses anonymised. Your quiz answers are never sent to Google Analytics.

How we use it

  • To give you your results, deliver the coaching you sign up for, and prepare your report.
  • To contact you about your assessment, sessions and program.
  • To send occasional sleep tips by email, which you can unsubscribe from at any time.
  • To improve our methods using anonymised data, only if you agree to this separately.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information or use it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run PRAPOSHAN (Google Workspace for secure storage and email, Razorpay and PayPal for payments, Calendly for scheduling, WhatsApp for messages), and with a doctor or psychologist only if you ask us to or agree to a referral. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep coaching records for as long as you are a client and for up to three years afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, at any time by emailing praposhanppc@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. These rights apply under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and under US state privacy laws where they apply to you. US residents: see also our consumer health data policy.

Age

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We store information in access-controlled accounts with two-factor authentication, share it only with people who need it to coach you, and review access regularly. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you promptly.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will be emailed to current clients.
