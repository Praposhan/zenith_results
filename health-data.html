Consumer health data privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

This policy applies to "consumer health data" as defined by laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's SB 370. It adds to our privacy policy.

The health data we collect

  • Answers about your sleep, energy, recovery, stress, mood and breathing during sleep (ZENITH Quiz and intake form).
  • Daily check-ins about your sleep and energy.
  • Wearable data you choose to share, such as sleep stages, heart rate and heart-rate variability.
  • Information you tell us in sessions, such as medications you mention.

Why we collect it

To give you your results, prepare your Deep Dive report, and deliver your coaching program. We do not use it for advertising.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, and from devices or apps you connect or export data from.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers listed in our privacy policy, which process it on our behalf, and a doctor or psychologist if you ask us to or agree to a referral. We never sell consumer health data.

Your rights

You can confirm whether we hold your health data, see it, delete it, or withdraw your consent, by emailing praposhanppc@gmail.com with the subject "Health data request". We respond within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our response, and we will reply to your appeal within 45 days.
