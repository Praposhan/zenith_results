How we work

Evidence-based, and adjusted every week.

  • CBT-I-informed coaching: your right sleep window, and the link between bed and sleep.
  • ACT-informed coaching: less effort, less struggle.
  • Body-clock coaching: light, wake time and activity timing.
  • Breathwork, yoga and mindfulness: helping a busy system switch off.
  • Weekly data reviews: progress against clinical norms and your own baseline.
