About Praposhan

Sleep is the lever nobody trains you to pull.

Praposhan exists because the people who most need better sleep are usually the ones with the least patience for generic wellness advice — and the least access to anyone who actually understands the science behind it.

What we're building

Most performance advice treats sleep as an afterthought — something to optimize once everything else is handled. We start from the opposite premise: sleep architecture is the foundation everything else sits on, and most high-performing professionals are running a serious deficit without realizing it.

Praposhan combines a clinically-grounded assessment (ZENITH) with structured coaching, so instead of generic advice, you get a precise picture of what's actually happening — and a program built around exactly that.

Who's behind it

Geethika Babu
Founder — Certified Dental Sleep Medicine Specialist & Sleep and Performance Coach

With expertise in sleep and dental science, nutrition, breathwork, and health coaching, Geethika has worked with over 2,000 clients on sleep and performance. She also serves as a Sleep Resident on Circle by CRED. Praposhan grew out of a simple observation from years of clinical practice: the gap between "sees a sleep specialist" and "gets real coaching support" is where most people fall through.
