About PRAPOSHAN

Sleep is the lever nobody trains you to pull.

Most advice treats sleep as something to fix once everything else is handled. We think that's backwards. Sleep is the foundation, and most high performers are running on a deficit they can't see.

Why we exist

Make the deficit visible. Find its cause. Fix it, measurably.

PRAPOSHAN brings together sleep science, data from the devices people already wear, and one-to-one coaching. Every client starts with measurement, every recommendation is tied to their own data, and every program is judged by whether the numbers change.
