Questions

Before you start

Is this medical treatment?

No. PRAPOSHAN provides evidence-informed sleep and performance coaching. It doesn't diagnose or treat medical conditions. If we see signs that something needs a doctor, we'll tell you and help you find the right one.

How much time does it take?

About two minutes each morning for 14 nights, plus two 45-minute sessions: your kickoff and your report call.

Do I need a wearable?

No. A tracker adds a daily objective view, but your ZENITH answers and morning check-ins work on their own.

What happens after the Deep Dive?

On your report call, our experts recommend whether Sleep to Rise is right for you, and whether Breath to Rise or Calm to Rise should be part of it. There's no obligation; the three changes are yours to keep.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your data is used only to coach you, stored securely and never sold. See our privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Because each Deep Dive is prepared personally for you, bookings are non-refundable once confirmed. If you have any concern, email us and we'll respond within 2–4 business days.
